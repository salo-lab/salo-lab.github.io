Maxime completed his PhD at the Laboratoire de Physique de l’ENS (LPENS) in Paris, where he studied how defects in lipid homeostasis and membrane organization contribute to caveolin-1- and seipin-associated lipodystrophies. His work focused on how altered lipid handling at the endoplasmic reticulum can propagate through cellular membranes, affecting Golgi organization, protein trafficking, and caveolae formation.

Maxime is a joint postdoctoral researcher in the Salo and Eero Castrén laboratories at the University of Helsinki. He will investigate how membrane architecture is associated with neuronal receptor activity using cryo-electron tomography (cryo-ET) and related tomography-based approaches.

Outside the lab, he enjoys swimming, collecting vinyl records, and watching basketball.
